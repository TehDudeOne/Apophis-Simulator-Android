# Third-party assets

## Planetary maps

The maps in `app/src/main/assets/*_map.jpg` are from [Solar System Scope's Solar Textures pack](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/). They are 2K equirectangular maps for the Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth, Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. The source states that the maps are based on NASA elevation and imagery data, with tuned colors and some fictional gap filling in unmapped areas.

Solar System Scope distributes the textures under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
